# CLAUDE.md — .github

**This repo is the public face of the organization.** `profile/README.md` renders at
`github.com/landseed-pbc` for anyone, logged in or not.

## Before you change anything here

- **This copy follows the live site; it never leads it.** Read `landseed.earth` and match it.
  In September 2026 this file was still leading with "mint Earth Credits — a standardized
  commodity" while the live site had zero occurrences of the phrase and titled itself
  "Landseed — Hardware, software, and data for the natural world". A positioning surface that
  drifts is worse than no surface, because strangers read it first and trust it.
- **Verify every link on the page before republishing it**, on bytes rather than status codes.
- **Voice rules apply** — `landseed-agents/identity/brand.md`. Direct, not breathless. Cite
  always. No moralising. No Web3 framing. No partner or founder names without approval.
- **Nothing confidential.** This is public. Not roadmap, not customers, not numbers that have not
  been published.

## Not the place for

Documentation. Org-wide knowledge belongs in
[`landseed-pbc/handbook`](https://github.com/landseed-pbc/handbook); repo-specific knowledge
belongs in that repo's own `CLAUDE.md`.
